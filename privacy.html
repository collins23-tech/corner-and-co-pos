Corner & Co. POS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy describes how the Corner & Co. point-of-sale register handles information. The store operating the register controls the sales and customer information entered into it.

Information the register handles

  • Store staff enter a PIN to access the register. The PIN is checked by the service; the register uses a secure session cookie after sign-in.
  • Product names, prices, stock quantities, and sale records are stored for the store. A sale record can include items, quantities, totals, tax, discounts, payment method, customer name, and an optional note.
  • Network information, such as an IP address, may be processed by the service to rate-limit failed sign-in attempts, secure the service, and troubleshoot errors.

How information is used and stored

Information is used to authenticate store staff, maintain inventory, calculate and record sales, and operate the register. The service uses Netlify for website hosting, serverless functions, and persistent data storage. Netlify may process technical request information to provide and protect those services.

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When those fonts are requested, Google may receive technical connection information such as an IP address.

Sales and inventory remain in the store's persistent register data while the service is active. The store operator is responsible for applicable record-retention requirements and can request assistance with data deletion through the support contact shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Payments

The register does not process payments, collect card numbers, or charge cards. Choosing a payment method records only whether the store marks a sale as cash, card, or other. Any actual payment is handled separately by the store.

Cookies, advertising, and security

The register uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie to keep an authenticated register session. It does not use advertising cookies or sell personal information. The website and its services use HTTPS and reasonable safeguards; no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This business register is intended for use by store staff and is not directed to children.

Privacy questions and requests

For questions or requests about information entered into a store's register, contact the store operator using the support contact shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the register or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.
